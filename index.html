Transparency by Construction

Every draw leaves a trail.

We do not ask you to take our word for it. We publish the evidence and the method, so anyone — participant or not — can independently reproduce the result.

Pool Frozen & Published

At the deadline, every eligible ticket number is sorted and hashed into a single snapshot digest. The list is published.

02

Seed Committed in Advance

A 256-bit server seed is generated at lock time. Its SHA-256 commitment is published before any draw occurs.

03

Selection by HMAC Chain

Each winner index is derived from HMAC-SHA256(seed, event|draw|prevHash) with rejection sampling, then reduced modulo the live pool size.

Trust & Security

Every entry has a record. Every result can be verified.

Verified entries only

A ticket exists only after a human has matched a payment record against a submission. No payment, no ticket.

Pool frozen at the deadline

Once the countdown ends, the eligible pool is hashed and locked at the database level. No ticket can be added or altered afterwards.

Winners cannot repeat

A database-level uniqueness constraint makes it impossible for the same ticket to be recorded as a winner of two prizes.

Completed draws are immutable

Finalised draw rows reject every update and delete, including from administrators. Corrections require a new, audited record.

Full audit trail

Every administrative action — approvals, rejections, locks, draws — is written to an append-only audit log.

Open verification

The verification page reproduces each draw from the published seed and pool, entirely in your browser.

Participate responsibly

This platform involves paid entries and monetary prizes. Participation is restricted to adults who meet the eligibility requirements published in the event rules. Never spend money you cannot afford to lose. If participation stops being entertainment, stop participating.

Operating a real-money lottery requires licensing, payment-provider approval, identity verification, taxation handling and age gating in most jurisdictions. This software is built so those controls can be configured and enforced — it does not by itself grant legal permission to operate.
